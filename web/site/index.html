How it plays

Read the bubbles

Passengers think out loud. Coffee, a cold drink, a hot meal or a sandwich. Get it to them before they give up on you.

Work the galley

Brew coffee, heat meals in the oven and grab cold items from the fridge. Leave something sitting too long and it spoils.

Plan four moves ahead

Click a seat or an appliance to queue it up. Your attendant can only remember four tasks, so pick your route wisely.

Keep the cabin happy

Every passenger's mood slides from happy to furious. Business class gets impatient a lot faster than economy.

Meet today's passengers

They have all paid for their ticket and they would all like something. Right now.

On your iPhone or iPad

No App Store needed. Put Plane Chaos on your home screen and it opens full screen, just like an app.

  1. Open the game in Safari: go to the game page.
  2. Tap the Share button in the toolbar. If you can't see it, tap ••• first.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen (scroll down the list if needed), then tap Add.
  4. Start it from the new icon and hold your device sideways.

Once it has loaded, the game also works offline, so it's ready for your next flight. On Android, open the menu ⋮ in Chrome and choose Add to Home screen or Install app.
